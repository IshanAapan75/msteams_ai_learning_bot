# Your AI Champions Bot: A Friendly Guide to Boosting Your AI Skills

Welcome to the AI Champions Bot! This is your personal AI coach right inside Microsoft Teams, designed to help you build practical AI skills, track your progress, and earn exciting rewards along the way. Think of it as your daily AI workout buddy.

---

## How to Talk to Your AI Coach: Interacting with the Bot

You'll find your AI Champions Bot in Microsoft Teams, either in a chat with you directly or in a team channel. Interacting with it is simple:

*   **Type Messages:** Just like talking to a teammate, you can type your messages or commands.
*   **Use Commands:** The bot understands specific commands, which usually start with a forward slash (`/`). These commands help you ask the bot to do something specific.

Here are some key commands you'll use:

*   **`/learning`:** Use this command to view your current AI learning module. It's your window into the lessons the bot has for you.
*   **`start quiz`:** Once you've completed a learning module, use this to begin a short quiz and test your understanding.

---

## Your Learning Journey: How the Bot Helps You Grow

The bot guides you through a structured path to AI fluency. Here’s how your journey unfolds:

### 1. Getting Started: Language Defaults to English
*   **Your First Interaction:** The bot now starts you off in English automatically, so you can begin learning right away. You can change this later from your dashboard if needed.
*   **Your First Learning Module:** As soon as you start chatting, the bot assigns your very first AI learning module. This module is immediately available for you to start exploring.

### 2. AI Learning Modules: Short Lessons for Big Impact
*   **What They Are:** These are short, focused lessons on key AI topics, designed to fit easily into your busy day. Each module helps you understand different aspects of AI and how to apply them.
*   **Accessing Your Module:** Simply type `/learning` to see your current module's details.
*   **Building Healthy Habits (Cooldowns):** The bot encourages consistent learning by introducing short breaks:
    *   **Quiz Cooldown (5 Minutes):** After you mark a learning module as complete, there's a short 5-minute wait before you can take the quiz. This helps you reflect on what you've learned.
    *   **Next Module Cooldown (18 Hours):** Once you've logged your usage for a module, the next module becomes available 18 hours later. This encourages you to practice what you've learned before moving on.

### 3. Quizzes: Test Your Knowledge
*   **What They Are:** Short, interactive tests that appear after you complete a learning module. They help reinforce your understanding of the module's content.
*   **When to Take Them:** After you mark a learning module as complete and have waited the 5-minute quiz cooldown, you can start the quiz.
*   **Starting a Quiz:** Type `start quiz` to begin.
*   **Logging Attempts:** The bot records all your quiz attempts, whether you pass or fail. This helps track your progress and identify areas for improvement.

### 4. Logging AI Usage: Showing Your Real-World Impact
*   **What It Is:** This is where you tell the bot about how you've used AI in your actual work tasks. It's a key part of demonstrating your AI fluency.
*   **When to Do It:** After you've successfully passed a quiz for a module and at least 1 hour has passed since passing the quiz, you can log your usage for that module.
*   **What It Asks:** The bot will ask you a few questions about your AI use, such as:
    *   What type of task did you use AI for?
    *   How much time did you save?
    *   How confident are you in the AI's output quality?
    *   (Optional) A brief description of how AI helped you.
*   **Why It's Important:** Logging your usage is essential! It earns you rewards and tells the bot you're ready for the next learning module.

## Earning Rewards: XP, Levels, and Badges

The AI Champions Bot rewards your dedication and progress with a fun and motivating reward system:

### 1. Experience Points (XP)
*   **What They Are:** XP are like points you collect for every meaningful AI-related activity.
*   **How You Earn Them:**
    *   **Completing Learning Modules:** Finishing a lesson.
    *   **Passing Quizzes:** Successfully answering quiz questions.
    *   **Logging AI Usage:** Recording how you used AI in your work.
*   **Purpose:** Your total XP determines your **Level** and helps you unlock **Badges**.

### 2. Levels: Your AI Fluency Ranks
*   **What They Are:** Levels are different stages of AI fluency you achieve as your total XP grows. You start as an "AI Rookie" and can progress to "AI Learner," "AI Explorer," and beyond, all the way to "AI Champion."
*   **How You Advance:** By continuously earning XP through your bot interactions and AI usage, you will automatically advance to higher levels. Each level signifies a greater command of AI skills.

### 3. Badges: Your AI Achievements
*   **What They Are:** Badges are special virtual awards you receive for achieving specific milestones or demonstrating consistent effort and skill. They are your digital trophies!
*   **How You Earn Them:**
    *   **Skill Badges:** Awarded for mastering specific AI skills or using AI in diverse ways (e.g., "Quick Learner" for completing many different AI task types).
    *   **Streak Badges:** Earned for consistently engaging with the bot and AI practice for a certain number of consecutive days (e.g., "7-Day Streak").
    *   **Productivity Badges:** Given for demonstrating significant time savings or efficiency gains through your AI usage (e.g., "Time Saver I" for saving 60+ minutes).
*   **Where They're Tracked:** All your earned badges are visible on your personal dashboard's "Badges" section.

## Key Bot Activities and Their Rewards (In Detail)

Here's a closer look at your core interactions with the bot and what you gain:

1.  **Activity: First-Time Setup (Automatic Language)**
    *   **Explanation:** When you first message the bot, it automatically sets your language to English so you can start instantly. You can adjust this later from the dashboard.
    *   **Reward:** You immediately get your *first AI learning module assigned*. This module is available for you to start right away, with no initial waiting period.

2.  **Activity: Completing a Learning Module**
    *   **Explanation:** You've read and understood the content of an AI learning lesson. You signal this to the bot.
    *   **Reward:** This action *unlocks the quiz* for that specific module.
    *   **Cooldown:** A 5-minute cooldown period begins. You'll need to wait for these 5 minutes to pass before the bot will allow you to `start quiz`.

3.  **Activity: Passing a Quiz**
    *   **Explanation:** You've successfully answered the questions in a quiz for a completed learning module.
    *   **Reward:** You earn **Experience Points (XP)** for your success! The bot also records the exact time you passed the quiz (`quizPassedAt`).
    *   **Attempts Logged:** Both passed and failed quiz attempts are recorded in your learning history.

4.  **Activity: Logging AI Usage (Submitting the Survey)**
    *   **Explanation:** After passing a quiz, you use the bot (or the dashboard) to record how you applied that AI learning in a real work scenario.
    *   **Timing:** You can only *log usage* for a module if at least 1 hour has passed since you passed its quiz.
    *   **Reward:** You earn more **Experience Points (XP)** based on your logged usage! Crucially, this action triggers the bot to assign your *next AI learning module*.
    *   **Next Module Cooldown:** The newly assigned module will become available to you after an 18-hour cooldown period, encouraging you to integrate your new skills before tackling the next lesson.

5.  **Activity: Maintaining a Streak**
    *   **Explanation:** Engaging with the bot or performing AI learning activities on consecutive days.
    *   **Reward:** Consistent daily interaction contributes to **streak-related XP bonuses** and can lead to earning **Streak Badges**.

By understanding these activities, you can effectively use the AI Champions Bot to continually improve your AI fluency and celebrate your achievements!
