# Your Personal AI Fluency Dashboard: A Simple Guide

Welcome to your personal dashboard! This is your central hub to see how you're progressing with your AI skills and how much impact you're making. Think of it like a fitness tracker, but for your AI abilities.

---

## Getting Around: The Navigation Menu

At the top of your screen, you'll find a navigation menu that helps you move between different parts of your AI journey. Here's what each item means:

*   **Personal Dashboard:** (You are here!) This is your main overview, showing you all your progress at a glance.
*   **Assessment:** Here, you can review your past quizzes. You won't take new quizzes here, but you'll see how you performed before.
*   **Log Usage:** This is where you record how you've used AI in your daily work. It's important for tracking your progress and earning rewards!
*   **Team Dashboard:** If you're part of a team, this section shows you how your team is collectively improving their AI skills.
*   **Leaderboard:** Want to see how you stack up against others? Check out the rankings of both individuals and teams here.
*   **Badges:** This is your trophy cabinet! See all the achievements you've earned and your progress towards new ones.

---

## Your Dashboard at a Glance: What You'll See

Your dashboard is designed to give you a quick, clear picture of your AI fluency. Here’s a tour of what each section tells you:

### 1. Welcome Message
*   **Purpose:** A friendly greeting that shows your name and when you were last active. It's a personal touch to start your day.

### 2. XP Progress (Experience Points)
*   **Purpose:** This section tracks your overall progress in the AI learning system.
*   **What is XP?** XP stands for Experience Points. You earn these by actively engaging with AI learning modules, passing quizzes, and logging your AI usage.
*   **Levels:** As you earn more XP, you'll advance through different levels (like "AI Rookie," "AI Explorer," etc.). Each level represents a higher stage of AI fluency.
*   **Progress Bar:** This visual bar shows you how close you are to reaching your next level. It clearly indicates your current XP and the target XP for the next milestone.

### 3. AI Fluency Score
*   **Purpose:** This is your overall score reflecting your skill and confidence in using AI. It's a holistic measure of your AI abilities.
*   **How it's measured (simplified):** Your fluency score considers various aspects of your AI usage, such as how confidently you use AI tools, your consistency, and your performance in assessments.
*   **How to improve it:** Regularly logging your AI usage and actively participating in learning activities will help boost this score.

### 4. Key Statistics
This section presents several important metrics about your AI journey:
*   **Confidence:** How confident you are in the quality of AI outputs you use.
*   **Current Streak:** The number of consecutive days you've engaged with AI learning or logged usage. Keeping a streak is great for building habits!
*   **Daily Avg XP:** The average Experience Points you've earned per day over a recent period.
*   **Total XP:** Your total Experience Points earned since you started.
*   **XP to Milestone:** How many more XP you need to reach your next significant achievement or level.

### 5. Recent Learning Wins
*   **Purpose:** A quick summary of your learning module progress.
*   **What you'll see:** It shows you how many modules you've started, how many you're currently working on, and how many you've successfully completed.

### 6. Quiz Activity
*   **Purpose:** Provides a snapshot of your performance in quizzes.
*   **What you'll see:** The total number of quiz attempts, how many you've passed, your overall pass rate, and your average score across all quizzes.

### 7. Recent Wins
*   **Purpose:** A simple log of your most recent achievements and how they contributed to your XP.
*   **What you'll see:** Details of recent events like passing a quiz, logging usage, or earning a badge, along with the XP awarded for that action.

---

## Reward Types

The system rewards your efforts in two main ways:

*   **Experience Points (XP):** These are numerical points you earn for completing tasks, learning modules, and passing quizzes. XP directly contributes to your overall level progression.
*   **Badges:** These are special virtual awards you receive for achieving specific milestones or demonstrating consistent behavior. Examples include badges for maintaining a long streak, saving a certain amount of time with AI, or completing a number of different AI tasks. They are your recognition for mastery!

---

## How to Use Your Dashboard Effectively

1.  **Start Here:** Always come to your "Personal Dashboard" to get an immediate overview of your progress.
2.  **Log Your Work:** Regularly use the "Log Usage" menu item to record how AI is helping you. This is crucial for tracking your impact and earning XP.
3.  **Review Assessments:** Visit the "Assessment" page to see your quiz history and understand your strengths and areas for improvement.
4.  **Track Achievements:** Head to the "Badges" page to celebrate your accomplishments and see what new badges you can aim for.
5.  **Stay Motivated:** Check the "Leaderboard" to see friendly competition, and if you're a manager, the "Team Dashboard" helps you track your team's collective growth.

Your dashboard is your personalized guide to building AI fluency and becoming an AI Champion!
